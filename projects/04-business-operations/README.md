# Case Study 04 - Business Operations for a Remote AI-Data Program

## Decision

How should a remote annotation and evaluation program move from pilot to production without sacrificing quality or privacy?

## Deliverables

- [Operating model](operating_model.md)
- `data/capacity_plan.csv` with forecast demand, handling time, required capacity and staffing gap
- `data/risk_register.csv` with owners, controls, triggers and escalation paths
- [Decision memo](decision_memo.md)

## Success measures

- documented entry and exit criteria for every phase;
- no production start before calibration passes;
- weekly capacity and quality reporting;
- explicit owners for risks and decisions;
- rapid route for instruction ambiguity, privacy and security issues.

## Modeled finding

The eight-week capacity model shows a negative staffing gap in every week, ranging from 1.50 to 2.80 FTE-equivalents. The correct decision is to add eligible capacity before release rather than absorb the gap through unplanned overtime or weaker quality sampling. The accompanying register assigns owners, controls and escalation triggers to eight risks, including two with critical impact.
